Reporting a problem

The quickest way is Settings ▸ Send Diagnostic Report in the app. It opens an email you can review before sending.

If you email directly, include your iPhone model, iOS version, and what happened. Remove anything sensitive from receipt photos before attaching them.

Common questions

An item or amount is wrong

Receipt reading can misread details. Tap any item, charge, or amount on Review Receipt to fix it, and check the total before sharing a split.

AI Reading isn’t available

AI Reading needs a connection and has a daily limit. Text Scanning works on your iPhone without either. You can switch in Settings.

How do I delete my data?

Go to Settings to clear receipt images or history. Deleting the app removes everything stored on your iPhone.
