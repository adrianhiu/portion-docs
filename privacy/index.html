Portion Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 October 2026

Portion helps you split receipts without a Portion account. It does not use advertising, analytics, or tracking.

Receipt reading

AI Reading uses Apple Private Cloud Compute or OpenAI to read receipt images. When using OpenAI, images pass through Portion’s Cloudflare backend with the original photo metadata removed. No account, device, contacts, or split-participant information is attached to the OpenAI request. Anything printed on the receipt remains visible.

The backend does not save receipt images or log their contents. It temporarily keeps reading results to recover interrupted requests. Apple, Cloudflare, and OpenAI process information under their own privacy policies; OpenAI may retain API data for abuse monitoring.

Turn off AI Reading in Settings to use Text Scanning entirely on your iPhone. Reading results may contain mistakes and remain editable.

Saved data

Receipt images, items, names, assignments, and split results are saved on your device. Supported iCloud features store certain information in your private iCloud account, which the developer cannot access.

Security

Apple App Attest verifies app installations. The backend keeps installation verification records and usage counters, and uses network information to limit abuse. These records are not sent to OpenAI or used for tracking.

Contacts

If you choose to select someone from Contacts, Portion receives only the information you explicitly select. Portion does not upload your address book to the developer.

Exchange rates

Portion may request current or historical exchange rates from the Frankfurter public API. These requests contain currency codes and an optional date. They do not include receipt images, names, split details, or other personal information.

Purchases

Optional support tips are processed by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive or store your payment-card details.

Support email

If you contact Portion Support, the information you choose to include—such as your email address, message, diagnostic report, or attachments—will be received and used only to respond to your request and troubleshoot the reported issue. Nothing is sent until you review the email and choose to send it.

Data retention and deletion

Local app data remains on your device until you delete it or remove the app. Data stored in your private iCloud account is managed through your Apple account and device settings. Support correspondence may be retained only as long as reasonably necessary to resolve your request.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to portionapp.support@gmail.com.
